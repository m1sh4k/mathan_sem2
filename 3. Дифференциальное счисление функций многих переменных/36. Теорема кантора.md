## Теорема Кантора

$$
\begin{align*}
&f(x):K \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R},\ f(x)\in C(K)\implies f(x)\text{ равномерно непр на }K \\
&\forall\varepsilon>0\exists\delta>0\forall x',x''\in K:||x'-x''||<\delta\implies |f(x')-f(x'')|<\varepsilon
\end{align*}
$$
### Докво (от противного)

$$
\begin{align*}
&\exists\varepsilon>0\forall\delta>0\exists x',x''\in K:||x'-x ''||<\delta \land |f(x')-f(x'')|>\varepsilon \\
&\{ \delta \}:=\left\{ \frac{1}{k} \right\} ,k\in \mathbb{N}\\
&||x_{k}''-x_{k}'||<\frac{1}{k}\quad |f(x'_{k})-f(x_{k}'')|\geq \varepsilon \\
&x'_{k_{j}}\xrightarrow[]{j\to \infty}x_{0}\in K \\
&||x'_{k}-x_{k}''||\xrightarrow{k\to \infty}0 \\
&\lim\limits_{ j \to \infty }x'_{k_{j}}=\lim\limits_{ j \to \infty } x''_{k_{j}}=:\xi \\
&\left.\begin{aligned}
&f(x''_{k_{j}})\xrightarrow{j\to \infty}f(\xi)\\
&f(x'_{k_{j}})\xrightarrow{j\to \infty}f(\xi) 
\end{aligned}\right\}\implies ||f(x'_{k_{j}})-f(x''_{k_{j}})||\xrightarrow{j\to \infty}0
\end{align*}
$$
