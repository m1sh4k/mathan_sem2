## Дифференцируемость

$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta f(x^{0}_{1},\dots,x_{n}^{0})\equiv f(x_{1}^{0}+\Delta x_{1},\dots,x_{n}^{0}+\Delta x_{n})-f(x_{1}^{0},\dots,x_{n}^{0})=A_{1}\Delta x_{1}+\dots+A_{n}\Delta x_{n}+\alpha(\Delta x_{1},\dots,\Delta x_{n})\cdot \sqrt{ (\Delta x_{1})^{2}+\dots+(\Delta x_{n})^{2} }
\end{align*}
$$
- $\alpha(\Delta x_{1},\dots,\Delta x_{n})\xrightarrow{\Delta x_{1},\dots,\Delta x_{n}\to 0}(0,..,0)$

### Условия

1. $f$ непрерывна в $(x_{1}^{0},\dots,x_{n}^{0})$
2. $i \in \mathbb{N}[1;n]$ существуют  конечные $\frac{\delta f}{\delta x_{i}}(x_{1}^{0},\dots,x_{n}^{0})$

##### непрерывность

$$
\begin{align*}
&f(x_{1}^{0}+\Delta x_{1},\dots,x_{n}^{0}+\Delta x_{n})=f(x_{1}^{0},\dots,x_{n}^{0})+A_{1}\Delta x_{1}+\dots+A_{n}\Delta x_{n}+ \alpha(\Delta x_{1},\dots,\Delta x_{n})\\
&\lim\limits_{ (\Delta x_{1},\dots,\Delta x_{n}) \to  (0,\dots,0)} f(x_{1}^{0}+\Delta x_{1},\dots,x_{n}^{0}+\Delta x_{n})=f(x_{1}^{0},\dots,x_{n}^{0}) \\
&\lim\limits_{ (x_{1},\dots x_{n}) \to (x_{1}^{0},\dots,x_{n}^{0}) } f(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})=f(x_{1}^{0},\dots,x_{n}^{0})
\end{align*}
$$
