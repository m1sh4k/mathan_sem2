$$
\begin{align*}
&df=f'_{x}dx \\
&d(f(x(t)))=f'_{x}x'_{t}dt=f'_{x}dx
\end{align*}
$$

$$
d^{2}f=d(df)=d(f'_{x}dx)=d(f'_{x})dx+f'_{x}d^{2}x=f''_{xx}dx^{2}+f'_{x}d^{2}x
$$

- $x$ независима — $d^{2}f=f''_{xx}dx^{2}$
- $x$ зависима — $d^{2}f=f''_{x}dx^{2}+f'_{x}d^{2}x$
  
### Для независимой
$$
\begin{align*}
df=f'_{x}dx=\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{ \partial f_{1} }{ \partial x_{1} } & \dots & \frac{ \partial f_{1} }{ \partial x_{m} }  \\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
\frac{ \partial f_{n} }{ \partial x_{1} }  & \dots & \frac{ \partial f_{n} }{ \partial x_{m} } 
\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}
dx_{1} \\
\vdots \\
dx_{m}
\end{pmatrix}
\end{align*}
$$

### Для зависимой

$$
\begin{align*}
d(f(x(t)))=(f(x(t)))|_{t}dt=(f|_{x}\cdot x|_{t})dt=f|_{x}\cdot (x|_{t}\ dt)=f|_{x}dx
\end{align*}
$$
