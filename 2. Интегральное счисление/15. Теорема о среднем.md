## Теорема о среднем
$$
\begin{align*} \\
&m:=\inf_{[a;b]}f(x);M:=\sup_{[a;b]}f(x)\\
&f(x),g(x)\in \mathcal{R}[a;b] \\
&g(x) \text{ сохраняет знак на } [a;b] \\
&\hline \\
&\int_{a}^{b}f(x)g(x)dx=\mu \int_{a}^bg(x)dx && \mu \in \left[ m;M \right] \\
&f(x)\in C[a;b]\implies \mu=f(\xi)&& \xi \in[a;b]
\end{align*}
$$


## Докво

$$
\begin{align*}
m\leq f(x)\leq M \bigg|\cdot g(x)\\
mg(x)\leq f(x)g(x)\leq Mg(x) \\
\int_{a}^{b}mg(x)dx\leq\int_{a}^{b} f(x)g(x)dx\leq\int_{a}^{b}Mg(x)dx  \\
m\leq \frac{\int\limits_{a}^{b}f(x)g(x)dx}{\int\limits_{a}^{b}g(x)dx}:=\mu\leq M
\end{align*}
$$
